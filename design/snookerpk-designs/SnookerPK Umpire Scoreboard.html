Umpire Scoreboard

The private screen the assigned referee uses to score a live singles frame ball-by-ball. It enforces the snooker sequence — reds then colours, then the colours-clearing phase — so only legal balls are tappable. Built for a tablet in landscape: big targets, the active player and the expected ball impossible to miss, and Undo always one tap away.

Live board— fully interactive · pot balls, end turns, foul, end frame

This board is real. Pot the on-ball to build a break, End Turn to switch players, hit Foul to open the foul flow, and End Frame to record the winner + high break. Illegal balls are dimmed and locked. Undo reverses any action.
