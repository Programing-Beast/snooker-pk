SnookerPK

A purpose-built design system for running snooker tournaments end to end — from data-dense draws and live scores to player profiles and, soon, a clean equipment store. Broadcast-sport energy, baize at its heart.

The thinking

  • Built from the table. The palette, the live-score voice and the recurring 7-ball motif all borrow from snooker’s own visual language — felt green, brass trophy gleam, spotlit arena.
  • One system, two surfaces. Tight, tabular, broadcast-grade screens for competition; airy and calm for the future shop — same tokens, no re-theme.
  • A platform, not one event. Tournaments can carry an “organized by / presented by” credit, so named organizers shine while SnookerPK stays the home brand.
  • Light first, dark ready. Every token has a dark-mode counterpart; the shell you’re looking at is that arena-dark direction.

What’s in here

  • 01 Two color palettes — pick one, or A/B them live with the toggle above.
  • 02 Wordmark & app-icon treatment in each palette.
  • 03 Type scale — Saira for the scoreboard voice, Hanken Grotesk for dense data.
  • 04 Core components — buttons, forms, tables, the match-row, badges, flag chips, tournament / stat / product cards.
  • 05 Spacing, radius & elevation foundations.
The theme toggle top-right re-colors every component below in real time — proof the system flexes without being rebuilt.
